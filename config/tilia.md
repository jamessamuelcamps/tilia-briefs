# About Tilia

Context for briefs. Edit as the product changes; briefs never invent feature details beyond this, they ask James instead.

- Tilia (Tilia Labs) is an unmoderated UX research platform focused on information architecture testing.
- Built by James, a product design lead, alongside a full-time design role.
- Positioning: fast, clear answers for lean product teams, without enterprise pricing (legacy tools often start around £150 a month) and without stitching together Figma, Google Forms and Excel.
- Methods: tree test, card sort, survey, preference test, five-second test, first-click test.
- Platform: synthesis, repository, workspace, screeners, consent, export.
- Recently shipped: survey settings, mixed studies, conditional questions, upload from markdown.
