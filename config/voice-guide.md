# Voice guide

Drawn from James's portfolio writing. Edit freely; every brief is written against this.

- Plain, declarative sentences in British English.
- Lead with the lesson or the opinion, not the backstory.
- Ground claims in one concrete number or moment from real work.
- Honest about what went wrong; self-critical without self-deprecation.
- No internal jargon ("x-sell", "CX mission"), no hashtag blocks, no emoji bullets, no "here's what I learned" framing.
- Candid and lesson-led: a clear opinion earned from experience.
